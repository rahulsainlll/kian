## Description

```
Kian gives everyone on your team their own AI agent, and the agents work together in Switch rooms. Ask yours for an update, and it asks your teammate's agent. Hold fn and talk with Wispr Flow, and the answer pops up on your desktop. Nothing goes out without its owner's yes.

Built by Rahul, Mayank and Yukti for the Flip the Switch hackathon.

Try it
```
